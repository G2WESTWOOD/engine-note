Engine Note

Real engines recorded in Engine Simulator while driving through the gears, played by your speed: clutch, shifts, rev-matching and the limiter.

  1. In the car's audio settings turn Immersive Sound on (Standard or High) if you have it, keep balance and fader centred, and set the EQ flat while you try it.
  2. Set the volume moderately and keep your eyes on the road.
