Engine Note

Synthesized engine through your car's speakers, matched to your GPS speed. Exhaust plays from the rear, intake and turbo from the front, low rumble through the subwoofer.

  1. Audio settings: turn Immersive Sound on (Standard or High) if your car has it. That's what moves exhaust to the rear speakers.
  2. Keep balance and fader centred.
  3. Set volume moderately and keep your eyes on the road.

Sound field: Surround encodes the exhaust so the car's upmixer sends it to the rear speakers. If it sounds hollow or phasey, your car isn't upmixing; switch to Stereo. Rev works whenever you're stopped. Keys: R to rev, Space for gas in Simulator.
